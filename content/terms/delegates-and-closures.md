title: Delegates and closures

  - csharp-type-fundamentals

A delegate is a type-safe reference to a method, and a closure is what a lambda becomes when it
captures a local from the enclosing scope — the compiler hoists that local onto the heap so it
outlives the stack frame it was written in.
