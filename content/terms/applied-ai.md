title: Applied AI
---

Using machine learning and large language models to solve a **business** problem rather than
to advance the models themselves — where they fit in a real workflow, what they cost, and the
governance a high-stakes domain forces around them. The engineering here is less about the
model and more about the **substrate**: retrieval, evaluation, human-in-the-loop checkpoints,
and the honest sorting of quick wins from moonshots. It is the frame for the "where would you
apply AI?" question a [[system-design]] conversation increasingly reaches for.
