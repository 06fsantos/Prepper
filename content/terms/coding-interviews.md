The coding rounds of the senior loop — the screen and the on-sites where you solve a problem
live. The algorithms are frequently the *same* ones a mid-level candidate gets; what changes is
the bar they are graded against. This topic is the meta-skill of the round — the graded signal
on top of a correct solution — not the data structures and algorithms themselves, which live
under [[big-o-notation]], [[hash-maps]], and the Problems.
