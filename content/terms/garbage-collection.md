The CLR's automatic memory manager: a generational, mark-and-sweep, compacting collector that
reclaims managed objects once nothing reachable refers to them. What a senior interview probes is
the mechanism underneath — where an object is born, what promotes it, when (and whether) its cleanup
runs, and how to keep it off the heap in the first place.
