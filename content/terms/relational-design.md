title: Relational schema design
---

The logical shape of the data: what a table is a set of, which columns identify a row, which
ones point at another table, and where a dependency has to be decomposed away. It is the part
of the design that is true before anything is stored or measured — and the part an interviewer
asks you to defend a decision in.
