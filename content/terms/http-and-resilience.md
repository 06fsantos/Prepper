title: HTTP & Resilience
---

Talking to a service that may be slow, may be down, and is never on the same machine. The topics
filed here cover the client that makes the call, the patterns that keep one failing dependency
from taking the caller down with it, and the tracing that lets you see where a request actually
spent its time.
