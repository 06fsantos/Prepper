title: Transactions and isolation
---

What the database promises when two sessions touch the same rows at once — and what it stops
promising in exchange for throughput. ACID names the four guarantees; the isolation levels are
the dial, each admitting a different set of anomalies, and SQL Server's row-versioning levels
are a second axis on the same dial.
