title: Generics
topic:
  - csharp-type-fundamentals
---

Parameterised types and methods — `List<T>`, `Dictionary<K,V>`, `where T : struct` — that let one
piece of code work over many types with full type safety. In C# they are a runtime feature, not
compile-time erasure, which is where the interesting interview questions live.
