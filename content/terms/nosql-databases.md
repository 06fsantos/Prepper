Stores that give up the relational model's fixed schema and general-purpose joins in exchange
for horizontal scale, a data layout shaped to one access pattern, and tunable consistency. The
notes filed here treat a specific store as a worked example of those trade-offs, not as a product
tour — what its partitioning buys, what its consistency knob costs, and when it beats a
[[the-relational-model-and-keys|relational]] default.
