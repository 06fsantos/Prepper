The leadership round of the senior loop — the non-coding conversation where you are asked to
tell stories about past work, and graded on what those stories prove about how you operate.
Its rubric is [[what-senior-means-as-a-level|the level itself]]: mentorship, ownership of
ambiguity, and conflict handled well are the scope claims that define senior, and this is the
round where you show they are true of you. The best-documented first-party rubric is Amazon's
Leadership Principles, and the structure for an answer is [[the-star-method|STAR]].
