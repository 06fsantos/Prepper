title: Mission
---

I am preparing for senior software-engineering interviews at companies that run a
four-stage loop: a coding screen, two coding rounds, and a system-design round, with
behavioural questions folded into each.

What I am optimising for, in order:

1. **Recall under pressure on the fundamentals.** I can reason about complexity when I
   have time; I want it to be automatic. Hash maps, sorting, graph traversal, the cost
   of the thing I just wrote.
2. **A vocabulary for system design.** I have built systems; I have not had to *narrate*
   the tradeoffs in forty-five minutes to someone who has.
3. **Not freezing.** The failure mode I have actually experienced is not ignorance, it is
   going quiet.

What I am deliberately not optimising for: exotic algorithms, competitive-programming
speed, and language trivia. Solutions in this vault are written in C#, which is the
language I will be interviewed in.
