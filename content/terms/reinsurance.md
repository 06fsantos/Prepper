title: Reinsurance
---

**Insurance for insurance companies.** A primary insurer (the **cedent**) transfers part of
the risk it has taken on to a reinsurer, which accepts it for a share of the premium — buying
the cedent capital relief, capacity, catastrophe protection and earnings stability. The
business runs as a repeatable placement lifecycle — submission → underwriting → pricing →
binding → booking → claims → settlement → renewal — that is heavily message-driven, which is
where a software system plugs in. See [[reinsurance-domain-primer]] for the vocabulary a
senior engineer needs to speak credibly to the business.
