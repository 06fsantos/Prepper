title: System design
---

The applied craft of the 45-minute design round: turning a deliberately vague prompt into a
set of components, surfacing the requirements and constraints that pin the problem down, and
selecting and justifying each building block against explicit trade-offs. It is where the
seniority bar is most visible, because the prompt is graded on *process* — how you decompose,
estimate, and defend a decision — rather than on landing one correct architecture. It sits on
top of [[distributed-systems]]: the theorems say what you cannot have at once, and system
design is choosing, out loud, what to give up for this workload.
