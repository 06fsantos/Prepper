title: Data Structures & Algorithms
---

The vocabulary an interviewer reaches for first: how a collection is laid out in memory, and
what that layout costs to search, insert and remove. The topics filed here are the concrete
structures and the notation for comparing them — pick a structure by the operation it has to be
fast at, and be able to say why in the language of growth rather than of stopwatches.
