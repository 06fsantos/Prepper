The `senior-interview-path` authoring queue in `.scratch/` is complete and the research note
[[what-do-senior-software-engineer-interviews-test]] is fully drawn into the Library. Seventeen
queued items produced thirteen Lessons, five Terms and five cheat sheets, two References
([[system-design-building-blocks]], [[amazons-16-leadership-principles]]) and two Plans
([[reading-order-for-the-system-design-round]] and [[reading-order-for-the-behavioral-round]], the
vault's fourth and fifth). The research note stays where it is and is not to be re-imported. This
closes the same way the four `incorporate-*` efforts did, but from a different kind of source: not a
teaching workspace of HTML lessons, but a single research investigation the queue turned into a note
map.

**This path is the reverse of every prior incorporation, and that is the finding.** The database,
concurrency, HTTPClient and C#-fundamentals efforts were each about *a .NET runtime*, and each closed
with a reading order whose closing section had to name which steps were language-specific. This one
is almost entirely language-agnostic — distributed-systems theorems, system-design craft, the graded
coding *signal* rather than more DSA, and the behavioral rubric — so both its Plans carry a "there is
no .NET half" section instead, and the behavioral Plan's analogue of that caveat is by *company*
(Amazon's LPs are specific; the scope-claim framing transfers) rather than by language. Three boundary
calls are worth recording for any future run here: **distributed-systems (why you cannot have
everything) was kept separate from system-design (how to run the 45-minute conversation)**, with the
applied Lessons linking back to the theorems rather than re-teaching them; **`coding-interviews` added
no algorithms** — the DSA was already in the vault, so the single Lesson there is the meta-signal
([[the-senior-coding-signal]]); and **`idempotency-and-safe-retries` was left where it was** under
`http-resilience` and linked, not re-authored, by the fallacies Lesson.

**No prior knowledge is recorded, and — as with 0004 — that is itself the finding.** This path was
authored from a research note, not from any dev-facing session, so there is no answered quiz, no
correction, and no disclosed depth to raise the floor with. Everything the mission optimises for that
this path serves — the *vocabulary for system design* and *not freezing* — is now on the page but
unproven in the dev. There is no further authoring queue: the next signal comes from the dev actually
working the quizzes and rehearsing the behavioral stories aloud, which is where evidence of what
landed will finally surface. Until then the whole senior loop is covered but untested, and the next
Record should be the first one in this cluster written from dev evidence rather than from a source.
