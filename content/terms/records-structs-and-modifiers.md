The layer above the value/reference distinction: the four ways to declare a type — `class`,
`struct`, `record`, `record struct` — and the modifiers (`readonly struct`, `ref struct`, and the
`in`/`ref`/`out` parameter modes) that tune how a value is copied, confined, or passed. Choosing
between them is a senior design judgement built on the mechanics of [[value-types-versus-reference-types]].
