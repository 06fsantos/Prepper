A set of nodes joined by edges — the structure behind grids, dependency chains, and networks
alike. Most interview graph questions turn on a single operation, **traversal**: visiting every
reachable node exactly once by breadth (BFS, a queue) or depth (DFS, a stack or recursion), which
costs [[big-o-notation|O(V+E)]] and lives or dies on a visited set.
