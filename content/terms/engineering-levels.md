title: Engineering levels
---

What a job title claims about **scope, autonomy, and impact** — and why the same coding or
design prompt is graded against a different bar at each level. The portable definition is not
the title, which varies between companies, but the progression framework behind it: how much
ambiguity you are expected to own, how far your work reaches, and whether you lead others
through it. This is the master lens for the whole senior interview — every round is really
asking whether the level's scope claim is true of you.
