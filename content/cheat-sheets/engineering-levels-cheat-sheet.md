- **"Senior" is a scope claim, not a skill claim.** Scope, autonomy, impact — not the title,
  which varies by company. Reason from the progression framework, not the label.
- **Same prompt, different bar.** The senior coding/design prompt is often the mid-level one.
  A clean correct solution that stops there is a *down-level* signal — it shows the skill, not
  the scope.
- **Mid → senior line, three parts:** autonomy (own the ambiguity *before* it is a project),
  complexity (own moderate-to-complex components end to end), impact (reaches past your own
  tasks to the team).
- **Senior adds three things over mid:** leadership (mentor, review others' work), ownership
  of ambiguity (specify the under-specified, defend under pushback), end-to-end delivery.
- **The ceiling:** org-wide, multi-team impact is **Staff+**, above senior. Senior's radius is
  your team and your project — claim that, don't overshoot.

The reach-for-it signal: every senior round is one question in disguise — *is the scope claim
true of this person?* Lead every answer with the trade-off you weighed and the decision you
owned, not just the result.
