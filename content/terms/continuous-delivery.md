title: Continuous delivery
---

How a change gets from a commit to production safely, quickly and routinely: integrating into
mainline continuously, keeping the software releasable at all times, the deployment pipeline every
change travels through, and the release strategies — blue-green, canary, rolling, feature flags,
expand-and-contract schema changes — that make a bad deploy cheap to undo. It is tool-agnostic: the
subject is the discipline, not any vendor's pipeline syntax.

It comes up as the "how do you ship this?", "how would you roll it out?" and "what if the deploy is
bad?" follow-ups in a [[system-design]] round, and in behavioural stories about shipping — and it is
what turns the independent deployability [[microservices]] promise into something a team actually
has.
