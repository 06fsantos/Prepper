title: Distributed systems
---

The theory under any system design: what a collection of machines connected by an unreliable
network can and cannot guarantee at once. The load-bearing results are impossibility
statements — CAP and PACELC on what a partition or plain latency forces you to give up,
consistency models on the spectrum between strong and eventual agreement, and consensus on how
nodes agree at all. A senior candidate is expected to *reason from* these rather than name
them, because they are what separates pattern-matching a design from defending its trade-offs.
